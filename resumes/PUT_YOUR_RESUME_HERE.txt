IMPORTANT: Place your resume.docx in this folder.

The job search tool will automatically detect any .docx file in this folder.
If your resume has a different name, update config/config.yaml:

  resume:
    folder: "./resumes"
    filename: "your-resume-name.docx"

The tool will use Azure AI Foundry to analyze your resume and extract:
- Skills and technologies
- Target job titles
- Years of experience
- Industry domains
- Search keywords

All of these are used to find and score matching jobs.
